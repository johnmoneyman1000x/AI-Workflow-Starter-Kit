# /research-synth

Turn raw research notes into insights you can act on.

## Usage

`/research-synth <notes file or pasted notes>`

## Steps

1. **Ingest.** Read all the notes. Don't summarize yet — just absorb.
2. **Extract observations.** Pull out discrete observations (what users did/said), each with a source tag. Separate observation from interpretation — this is where most syntheses go wrong.
3. **Cluster.** Group observations into 3–6 themes. Name each theme as a tension or pattern, not a topic ("Users want control over X, but the UI hides it" — not "UI feedback").
4. **Insights.** For each theme, write the insight: the non-obvious truth it reveals. One or two sentences. If it's obvious, it's not an insight — dig deeper.
5. **Opportunities.** Convert insights into opportunity statements: "How might we...?" Rank by (evidence strength × potential impact).
6. **Open questions.** What the research didn't answer. Be honest about sample limits.

## Output

A structured doc: observations → themes → insights → ranked opportunities → open questions. Quotable, skimmable, no fluff. End with the single most important takeaway in one sentence.

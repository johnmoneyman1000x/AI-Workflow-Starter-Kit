# /rundown

One numbered list of everything open in your work life: what you owe people, what they owe you.

## Usage

`/rundown`

Reads your email, calendar, meeting notes, and project trackers. Built for mornings, useful whenever the day goes blurry.

## How it works

1. **Gather.** Pull recent email (inbox + sent), the next two business days of calendar with docs for those meetings, recent meeting notes, chat threads, and tracker updates.
2. **Hunt the two ledgers.** Threads gone quiet where you're waiting on someone (5+ days, no reply). Promises others made to you. Promises you made to others.
3. **Be honest about blind spots.** If a source can't be reached, say which one and what to check manually instead. Never present a partial sweep as complete.
4. **Write it up.** A two-sentence read on the day, then a single numbered list — max 10 items, ordered by urgency — grouped under Inbox, Calendar, and Elsewhere. Close with a short "can wait" section.

## Rules

- Verify before you list. Open the thread; if you can't confirm it, it stays out.
- Only things that need movement in the next two business days, or decisions with real weight. Skip FYIs, done items, and robot mail.
- Hands off: suggest reply drafts if asked, but never send, file, or change anything on your own.

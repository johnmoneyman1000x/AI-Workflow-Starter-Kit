# /review-design

Adversarial design critique of any UI — a screenshot, a URL, or the current project.

## Usage

`/review-design <screenshot path, URL, or "current project">`

## Steps

1. **Look first.** Examine the UI carefully before judging. Note what it's trying to do and who it's for.
2. **Score it.** Rate 1–5 on each: hierarchy, typography, spacing/rhythm, color restraint, copy quality, mobile behavior, states (loading/empty/error).
3. **Find the 3 biggest problems.** Not nits — the three things that most hurt comprehension or trust. Be specific: name the element, say what's wrong, say why it matters.
4. **Propose fixes.** For each problem: the concrete change, ranked by impact/effort. Include copy rewrites where copy is the problem.
5. **Call out what's working.** Two things to keep. Critique without this is just complaining.

## Rules

- Be the principal designer in the crit, not a cheerleader. "Looks good" is not a finding.
- Judge against the taste standards in CLAUDE.md. Flag any AI-slop signatures by name.
- Separate taste disagreements ("I'd choose a different typeface") from real defects ("the primary CTA is invisible on mobile") — label which is which.

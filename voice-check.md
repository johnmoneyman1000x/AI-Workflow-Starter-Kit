# /voice-check

Strip AI writing patterns without flattening your voice.

## Usage

`/voice-check <paste your draft>`

Add `--flag` to only flag the patterns without rewriting: `/voice-check --flag <draft>`

## Steps

1. **Read twice.** Once for what it's saying, once for how it sounds.
2. **Flag the tells.** Em-dash crutches, "X, not Y" pairings, "delve / unlock / tapestry" vocabulary, rule-of-three triplets, throat-clearing intros, hedged conclusions.
3. **Rewrite the flagged spots** in the writer's own cadence — short sentences where they write short, fragments where they use fragments.
4. **Report the changes.** One line per change: what moved and why.

## Rules

- Preserve the writer's quirks. If they swear, keep the swearing. Voice beats polish.
- Never add ideas. This is an edit pass, not a co-write.
- If a sentence sounds like anyone could have written it, rewrite it until it sounds like them.

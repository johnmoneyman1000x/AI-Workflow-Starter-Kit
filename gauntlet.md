# /gauntlet

Put a decision through three hard rounds before you commit.

## Usage

`/gauntlet <the decision, one sentence>`

Example: `/gauntlet should I launch the starter kit on HN before the newsletter post is live`

## How it works

1. **State it cleanly.** One sentence: the decision and what "yes" costs. Trivial call? Skip the rounds and just answer.
2. **Take a first position.** Your genuine gut read, on the record before the rounds begin.
3. **Round one — the opposite case.** Steelman the strongest argument against your position. No strawmen.
4. **Round two — the pre-mortem.** Assume it failed six months from now. What broke, and what was the earliest warning sign you ignored?
5. **Round three — the smallest version.** What's the cheapest shippable test of this decision, and what would you measure?
6. **Make the call.** One recommendation, one concrete next move, and the single biggest open question if one remains.

## Rules

- Each round must engage the previous ones — no isolated essays.
- If the answer is obvious after round one, stop and say so. Don't manufacture drama.
- A specialist round (pricing, legal, technical) only earns its place if it would change the call.
- The ending is always one move, not a menu of options.

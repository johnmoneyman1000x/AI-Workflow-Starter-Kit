# /pulse

Take the temperature of the internet on any topic — every claim linked to its source.

## Usage

`/pulse <topic> [window, default: last 30 days]`

Example: `/pulse claude code subagents, last 30 days`

## How it works

1. **Cast a wide net.** Search Reddit, Hacker News, YouTube, forums, and the open web inside the time window.
2. **Cluster into claims.** Group what you find by what people are asserting, not where you found it.
3. **Attach a source to each claim.** Who said it, where, link it.
4. **Map the terrain.** Where's the consensus, where's the fight, what's still genuinely unknown.

## Rules

- No link, no claim. Unsourced assertions don't make the cut.
- Quote when the exact words matter; paraphrase when they don't.
- Report the gaps too — what you looked for and couldn't find is part of the answer.
- Fresh beats old: weight recent takes over ancient ones.

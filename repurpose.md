# /repurpose

Turn one piece of content into native posts for every platform.

## Usage

`/repurpose <paste source> --for x,linkedin,threads,notes`

Example: `/repurpose <my latest issue> --for x,linkedin,notes`

## Steps

1. **Extract the 2–3 sharpest ideas** from the source. One idea per post — don't cram.
2. **Draft each platform natively:**
   - **X:** hook-first thread, one idea per post, no hashtags.
   - **LinkedIn:** story-led, generous line breaks, ends with a question.
   - **Threads:** casual, conversational, lowercase energy is fine.
   - **Substack Notes:** curiosity gap + link, under 100 words.
3. **Keep the author's voice** consistent across all versions.

## Rules

- Never cross-post identical copy. Each platform gets its own draft.
- Cut 30% more than feels comfortable. Feeds reward brevity.
- Drafts only — the human hits send. This command never publishes.
